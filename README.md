## ✨ Key Features

### ⏱️ Precision Timer
Track every study session with accuracy. The built-in timer lets you log hours for each subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) and automatically records your daily progress. No more guessing how much you studied—JEE_Tracker keeps exact numbers.

### 📅 Smart Planner
Plan your daily, weekly, and monthly study schedules. Set targets per subject, mark completed tasks, and let JEE_Tracker remind you of upcoming chapters and revisions. The planner adapts to your pace.

### 📚 Complete Syllabus Tracker
The entire JEE 2027 syllabus (Class 11 & 12 PCM) is pre-loaded. Check off topics as you finish them. Visual progress bars show how much of each subject you've covered—green for done, yellow for in progress, gray for pending.

### 📊 Mock Test Analytics
After each mock test, enter your scores. JEE_Tracker generates easy-to-understand charts showing:
- Score trends over time
- Subject-wise performance breakdown
- Weak areas that need more attention
- Percentile estimates based on your history

### 📈 Study Reports
Get weekly and monthly reports summarizing your study hours, syllabus completion, mock scores, and consistency. Perfect for reviewing what's working and what needs adjustment.

### 🔄 Cross-Device Sync (Firebase)
Study on your laptop? Check progress on your phone? JEE_Tracker offers optional **Firebase sync** so your data stays up-to-date across all devices. This is completely optional—everything works offline by default.

### 📴 Offline-First
All features work **without internet**. Your data is stored locally on your device. Sync only happens if you enable it and have a connection.

### 📱 Installable PWA
Once installed, JEE_Tracker runs like a native app—full-screen, no browser tabs, fast launching. It even works offline after the first load.

## 📖 How to Use JEE_Tracker

### First-Time Setup
1. Open the application.
2. Set your target exam date (default: May 2027).
3. Choose your subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics are standard, but you can add more).
4. Start your first study session with the timer!

### Daily Workflow
- **Morning:** Open the Planner, review today's tasks.
- **During Study:** Start the timer, select the subject, study, then stop the timer.
- **Evening:** Update syllabus progress, log any mock test scores.
- **Weekly:** Check the Study Reports section to see your consistency.

### Mock Test Entry
- Go to **Analytics** → **Add Mock Test**.
- Enter the test name, date, subjects attempted, and marks obtained.
- JEE_Tracker automatically updates charts.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

### ❓ JEE_Tracker doesn't open?
- Make sure you're using a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
- Try right-clicking the index.html file → **Open with** → choose your browser.
- If the file is inside a ZIP, extract it first before opening.

### ❓ Can I use it on my phone?
Yes! Open the same file on your phone's browser, or if you've enabled Firebase sync, install the PWA on multiple devices.

### ❓ How do I enable Firebase sync?
- Go to **Settings** → **Sync**.
- Create a free Firebase project (tutorials available online) and paste your config keys.
- Or skip it—JEE_Tracker works 100% offline without it.

### ❓ Will my data be lost if I clear browser data?
Yes—unless you use sync. We recommend enabling Firebase sync for safe backups.

### ❓ Can I prioritize subjects?
Absolutely. In the Planner, drag tasks to reorder them. The Timer also lets you label each session with a subject.

### ❓ Is this for JEE 2028 too?
Yes! The syllabus and features are adaptable. Just change your target date in Settings.
